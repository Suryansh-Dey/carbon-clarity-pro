PRIVATE-SECTOR PROJECTS

### IFC Performance Standards

The eight standards guide IFC clients in identifying and managing project impacts, including engagement and disclosure. They provide a structured reference for due diligence and environmental and social management systems.

1. **PS1** — Risk assessment and management
2. **PS2** — Worker rights and working conditions
3. **PS3** — Resource efficiency and pollution prevention
4. **PS4** — Community health, safety and security
5. **PS5** — Land acquisition and displacement
6. **PS6** — Biodiversity and living natural resources
7. **PS7** — Indigenous Peoples
8. **PS8** — Cultural heritage

[Read IFC Performance Standards](https://www.ifc.org/en/insights-reports/2012/ifc-performance-standards)

2012 standards reference. IFC is updating its Sustainability Framework; confirm the applicable version for each engagement.

WORLD BANK INVESTMENT PROJECT FINANCING

### Environmental and Social Framework

The ESF combines a development vision, borrower standards and Bank policy. It applies to World Bank Investment Project Financing projects initiated on or after 1 October 2018, using risk-proportionate oversight and adaptive management.

#### The Environmental and Social Standards (ESS)

1. **ESS1** — Project risk assessment, management and monitoring
2. **ESS2** — Labour and working conditions
3. **ESS3** — Resource use and pollution management
4. **ESS4** — Community health and safety
5. **ESS5** — Land-use restrictions, acquisition and resettlement
6. **ESS6** — Biodiversity and natural resource stewardship
7. **ESS7** — Indigenous Peoples and specified historically underserved traditional communities
8. **ESS8** — Cultural heritage
9. **ESS9** — Financial intermediaries and portfolio risk
10. **ESS10** — Stakeholder engagement and information disclosure

[Explore the World Bank ESF](https://www.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/environmental-and-social-framework)

Summarised topics. [Read the full ESS requirements](https://www.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/environmental-and-social-framework/brief/environmental-and-social-standards).

### From safeguards to resilience and adaptation.

Risk assessment, responsive management and stakeholder feedback help projects adapt as conditions change. The ESF also supports institutional capacity, transparent consultation and accessible grievance mechanisms.

#### Governments & public institutions

Use clear responsibilities and evidence to strengthen project oversight and implementation capacity.

#### Development institutions & financiers

Connect risk screening with financing decisions, action plans and portfolio monitoring.

#### Companies & project developers

Address operational gaps early and integrate safeguards into project design and delivery.

#### People & ecosystems

Build protection, inclusion and meaningful participation into the development process.

**CliSo’s practical application:** assess physical climate exposure, community vulnerability and resource dependencies; prioritise protective measures; assign owners, budgets and indicators; then review outcomes as project conditions evolve.

Framework alignment supports due diligence; it does not certify compliance or guarantee funding. Applicable law, lender conditions and project-specific requirements must be assessed separately. IFC and World Bank requirements are distinct and are not interchangeable.

CLIMATE / CLISO PERSPECTIVE

### From an emissions inventory
to a practical action plan.

An inventory is a starting point. Decisions become actionable when emissions hotspots are connected to ownership, investment and measurable progress.

Read perspective

Start by defining organisational boundaries and checking activity data across Scope 1, 2 and 3. Identify material hotspots, then compare reduction measures using implementation time, capital costs, projected savings and operational constraints. Assign owners, establish evidence requirements and review performance as new data becomes available. Separate estimated reductions from verified results to keep the roadmap credible.
